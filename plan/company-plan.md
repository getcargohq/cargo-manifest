[[global/company]]). Replace every section with your own.

## The one goal

Reach $8M ARR by 2026-12-31, up from $5.2M on 2026-06-30.

One goal. If a piece of work does not move this number, it is not on the plan.

## The forecast

| Source | Contribution to the $2.8M | Assumption |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Expansion of existing accounts | $1.1M | 18 percent net expansion, in line with H1 |
| Inbound (content plus PLG trial) | $0.9M | Trial-to-paid holds at 22 percent, volume up 40 percent |
| Outbound to the mid-market ICP | $0.8M | New motion, the biggest bet and the biggest risk |

The forecast is the honest part: two of the three lines are extrapolation, one
is a bet we have not proven yet. That is what makes outbound the focus.

## The three moves

1. **Stand up outbound to mid-market B2B SaaS.** We have never run it. See
   [[icp/mid-market-b2b-saas]] and the initiative `initiatives/outbound-motion.md`.
2. **Make the trial convert.** Activation is the leak: 22 percent of trials pay,
   and the ones that do all hit the cohort report in week one.
3. **Expand where we already won.** Multi-team land-and-expand inside existing
   accounts, starting with the ones that match [[proof/case-study-initech]].

Anything not on this list is a distraction until 2027 planning.
