## Firmographic

- Industry: B2B SaaS, product-led or hybrid sales motion
- Size: 50 to 500 employees
- Geo: North America and Western Europe
- Revenue: $5M to $50M ARR
- Tech stack: modern data-adjacent stack (Segment or RudderStack, a CRM, a warehouse they underuse)

## Pains

- Growth decisions made on gut feel because analytics is stitched together
- Data team is a shared bottleneck; growth waits weeks for answers
- Tool sprawl: five dashboards, no single view of the funnel

## Triggers

Cross-ref [[signal/hiring-gtm-engineer]]: hiring for growth or GTM
engineering roles is the strongest observed trigger.

- New VP Growth or first growth hire in the last 90 days
- Series A or B raised in the last 6 months
- Migrating CRM or CDP

## Disqualifiers

- Under 30 employees (no budget owner, trial churns)
- Enterprise with a mature data team (they build in-house)
- Non-SaaS business models

## How to identify

- Filters: SaaS keywords + headcount 50-500 + funding events
- Intent: job posts mentioning growth/GTM engineering, analytics migration
