## The customer

Initech, 240-person B2B SaaS, hybrid PLG and sales motion. Perfect
[[icp/mid-market-b2b-saas]] fit: one overloaded data analyst serving the
whole company.

## The numbers

- Question-to-answer time: 9 days to same-day
- Activation rate: +18 percent in two quarters
- Data-team tickets from growth: down 70 percent

## The quote

"We stopped arguing about whose numbers were right and started shipping
experiments." VP Growth, Initech.
