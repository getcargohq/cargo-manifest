## Role

- Title: Head of Growth, VP Growth, sometimes Head of Marketing at smaller companies
- Seniority: first or second growth leader in the company
- Function: owns the pipeline or signup number end to end
- Reports to: CEO or CRO

## KPIs

- Qualified pipeline or activated signups per quarter
- CAC payback
- Conversion rates at each funnel stage

## Pains

- Every funnel question becomes a ticket for the data team
- Cannot tell which campaigns produce revenue, only which produce clicks
- Board asks cohort questions the current dashboards cannot answer

## Motivations

- Wants to be the person who made growth an engineering discipline
- Buys tools that remove dependencies, not tools that add process

## Day-to-day

Tuesday: standup, funnel review with stale numbers, two campaign launches,
an hour lost reconciling CRM and analytics counts, evening spent in a
spreadsheet answering the CEO's cohort question.

## Preferred channels

- Peer communities and word of mouth first
- Technical blog posts with real numbers
- Ignores cold email that opens with flattery; responds to specific,
  researched relevance (see [[objection/we-already-have-dashboards]])
