## What it is

The account publishes a job post for a growth engineer, GTM engineer, or
first data hire reporting into growth.

## What it predicts

They are building the motion our product powers. In the last two quarters,
accounts showing this signal converted from first touch to opportunity at
roughly three times the baseline rate.

## How we detect it

Job-post monitoring on the TAM list (see `infra/` for the monitoring play
wiring). Keywords: "growth engineer", "GTM engineer", "revenue operations"
plus analytics stack mentions.

## What to do when it fires

Route into [[play/inbound-flow]] with the signal attached; the first-touch
message references the role they are hiring for, never the fact that we saw
the job post.
