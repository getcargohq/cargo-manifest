## What we sell

Acme Analytics is product analytics for B2B SaaS teams. It answers "which
users are about to convert, churn, or expand" without requiring a data team.

## Pricing

- Growth: $500/mo, up to 50k tracked users.
- Scale: $2,000/mo, unlimited users, SSO, audit log.
- Annual only above Growth. No free tier; 14-day trial.

## Tone of voice

Plain, technical, confident. Short sentences. No hype adjectives, no em
dashes. We show numbers before adjectives.

## Positioning in one line

The analytics layer B2B SaaS teams run their growth motion on, from first
touch to expansion, without a data team.
