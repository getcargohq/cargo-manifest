## The objection

"We already have dashboards in [BI tool / spreadsheet stack]. Why would we
add another analytics product?"

## What it usually means

Dashboards exist but every new question still requires the data team. The
pain is the question-to-answer latency, not the absence of charts.

## How to handle it

Ask: "How long does it take to answer a question your current dashboards
were not built for?" Then show the cohort-question demo (under 2 minutes,
no data team). Back it with [[proof/case-study-initech]].

## What not to do

Do not do a feature-by-feature comparison against their BI tool. We lose
that framing and it misses the point.
